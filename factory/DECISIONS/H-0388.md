- Question: the accept arm asks *is the fragment on the promised row, and only on that row* — #440
  settled the second half, and neither rule asked where the quotation stops — so containment answers
  both for any fragment, including one cut in the middle of a word. Five rows of `factory/DECISIONS.md`

  head of one shell substitution out of its assignment (53 characters of a 62-character row), and the
  nine characters they leave off are the word `question`, which each of those five rows then names in
  its own prose. `628e403` wrote the default inside the substitution as `needs-human`; `431b24b`
  changed it to `question` and moved these pointers onto the assignment's new line, and that commit's
  own diff is the damage: it rewrote six rows carrying the whole `:-needs-human}` quotation and shipped
  five of them ending at `:-`, keeping one whole. So the gate was green on both sides, because the
  surviving prefix is identical for every value the substitution has ever carried.
  `factory/factory.sh:342` "-name 'triage-stderr.*'" carries the same shape at a quotation's other end:
  the row says `-name` and the quote opens on `name`. The ticket's requirement is that the shipped
  predicate answer red for that pair; where to put the bound was this unit's to measure.
- Options: repair the six quotations and teach the gate nothing | refuse a cut quotation as its own
  must-be-zero bucket, alphabet = word characters | the same bucket with `:` and `.` added to the
  alphabet | fold the cut into the #440 arm as another `indeterminate` | raise `MIN_ANCHOR` so a short
  quotation cannot pass
- Chose: refuse a cut quotation as its own must-be-zero bucket, alphabet = word characters
- Why: the defect is that a green pointer cannot fail, and only the character touching the quotation
  says whether it is finished. `scripts/check-decisions.py:1689` "if want in region:" is the accept test
  and it is indifferent to where the fragment stops, so the new arm reads the same joined region right
  after that test, `scripts/check-decisions.py:1697` "cut = anchor_cut(want, region)", and refuses on
  the character beside the quote: `scripts/check-decisions.py:3806` "ANCHOR_TOKEN_RUN = frozenset" is
  the alphabet, `scripts/check-decisions.py:3811` "def anchor_cut(want, region)" is the cut and what it
  dropped, and the refusal
  at `scripts/check-decisions.py:1702` "but the quotation stops inside a token" prints both the place
  and the characters left behind. Measured blast radius: the new checker run against the untouched
  `origin/main` corpus refuses 6 rows out of the 577 anchored and resolvable among 1,663 scanned — the
  five above and `factory/factory.sh:342` "-name 'triage-stderr.*'" — and each of the six was read and
  is a real cut.
  The six repairs are the whole corpus work: five rows gain the nine characters — every one of them
  counted from the shipped diff at 53 characters before and 62 after — and the sixth moves the `-`
  inside the quotation, length unchanged at 23; the longer fragment was re-checked against #440's rule
  each time, and the substitution occurs exactly once in `factory/common.sh`, so no repair lands on a
  rival row. Why the
  other four lost, each measured. A length floor cannot express the defect: the floor is
  `MIN_ANCHOR = 16`, the corpus's whole quotations run from 16 to 101 characters, and the two cut
  quotations are 53 and 22 — one far above the floor, one near it, so no number of characters separates
  a cut from a whole quote. Widening the alphabet to `:` costs 29 further quotations and `.` costs 22,
  their union 50, `/` and `-` one row each, and every one of them a quote ending at a completed word that
  the cited line merely follows with that punctuation (`AGENTS.md:84` "ask the supervisor's journal"
  against a row going on `journal: run`); each figure counts a row on any cut match, so it is that
  widening's upper bound, and the conservative arm below can only lower it. None of it buys the boundary
  the ticket asked for: the substitution it was filed on is refused already, because the character after
  the quote is a `q`. Folding the cut into
  `indeterminate` puts a *judged* row in the bucket for rows nothing can judge, and that bucket is
  counted rather than refused, so a cut would rot the way the 41 unjudgeable pointers of H-0383 rot; a
  cut is also the repairable class — finishing the quotation is the whole fix — which is why `midtoken`
  is refused on sight, holds the ratchet while one exists, and never enters `POINTER_DEBT`.
  Repair-without-teaching was rejected by the ticket itself: `431b24b` is the counterexample, a commit
  that re-aimed pointers correctly by line number and damaged the quoted span, with nothing in
  `make verify` able to see the span. Conservative on multiplicity, as #440 is: a fragment matching the
  cited text more than once passes if ANY match is whole, because lengthening whichever copy the check
  cannot tell is advice it must not give.
- Confidence: high on the predicate, medium on the alphabet's future price. The predicate carries 7 new
  must-fail legs and 10 new must-pass legs — `--self-test` moved from 98/68 to 105/78 — and the legs
  read the fixture's two preconditions off `anchor_cut` itself before any refusal is asserted, which is
  the vacuous-plant failure #495 names as its own stop condition: a corpus of nothing but cuts would
  otherwise pass every leg with a predicate that fires on everything. What is medium is the alphabet: a
  quotation that legitimately ends at a `:` inside an expression stays green, and a cut word sitting
  beyond that colon still passes today. It is bounded, because the rule refuses only a token
  continuation touching the quotation, and the 50 rows that adding `:` or `.` would refuse are all of
  that shape.
- What lost: repair-without-teaching; a widened token alphabet (50 re-quotations for no new boundary);
  `indeterminate` as the home for a judged row; a `MIN_ANCHOR` raise (inexpressible); counting `midtoken`
  into `POINTER_DEBT` (it would teach lowering the constant to hide a cut); and the conservatism's cost —
  a fragment with one whole copy and one cut copy stays green. Disclosed beside them, because it is a
  cost of the repairs and not of the rule: the five rows now quote the substitution through `question}`,
  so they read as claims about the *value*, and a future default change reddens five rows where today it
  reddens none. Two slips in getting here, both caught by the gate rather than by luck: the re-aim map
